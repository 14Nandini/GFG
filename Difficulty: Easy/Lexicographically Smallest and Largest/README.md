You need to find the lexicographically smallest and largest.</span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>s[] = [a , ab , abc]

<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> s[] = [abc , abc]
<strong>Output:</strong> [abc, abc]
